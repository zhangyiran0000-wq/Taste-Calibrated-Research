Trace the problems behind a paper

Give it one paper. It maps who solved whose problem, checks which works hold up, and writes a report you can share.

With a limit, works from other fields (a generic diffusion model, a backbone) are listed as borrowed tools. They are not put on the map, read or audited, and do not use up the paper budget.

They go on the map with the seed, even outside the field limit, and the map grows from them too. They count towards Max papers.

Title or linkScore 0–1Why

Papers you liked or disliked, with your own score of their contribution from 0 to 1 and a line on why. Your taste is calibrated on them: with 3 or more, the report also gets its taste calibration, which runs after the map and takes about an hour more. They never change how the papers are read.
