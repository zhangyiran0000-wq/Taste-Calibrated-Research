Trace the problems behind a paper

Give it one paper. It maps who solved whose problem, checks which works hold up, and writes a report you can share.

With a limit, works from other fields (a generic diffusion model, a backbone) are listed as borrowed tools. They are not put on the map, read or audited, and do not use up the paper budget.
