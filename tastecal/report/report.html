          How this map was grown

          Expansion follows problems, not raw citation counts, so the map stays small enough to read.

          1. Read the seedReduce it to what it inherits, the move it makes, its key insight, and the limitations it leaves. The limitations open the problem ledger.
          2. Open citations by how they are citedBackward: keep references the seed cites when framing its problem. Forward: keep citers whose citing sentence fixes, extends or challenges it. Drop mere users.
          3. Search by problemEach open problem becomes a query over papers and the web, which finds work that tackles it without citing the seed, and blogs.
          4. Update the ledger, repeatNew works close problems and open new ones. Stop when the budget is full or rounds stop producing new problems.
          5. SynthesizeGroup problems into threads, ask "why?" down to a root, merge shared roots into driving forces, then test them against later work.
