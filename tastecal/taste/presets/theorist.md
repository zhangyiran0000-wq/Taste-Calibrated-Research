# Theorist

A researcher who wants to know why something works, and when it would stop working.

## Problems I care about
- The mechanism behind a gain, and the conditions under which it fails.
- Results that overturn an accepted explanation.

## Ideas I like
- A derivation or a mechanism that makes a second, testable prediction.
- A simple experiment that breaks a common belief.
- Analyses that show where a gain really comes from.

## Ideas I usually skip
- Methods justified only by an analogy ("inspired by the brain") with no bound on when they help.
- Engineering combinations with no new idea behind them.
- Claims of generality that the experiments do not test.

## Papers I liked
- Understanding deep learning requires rethinking generalization (2016): one simple experiment (random labels) broke an accepted explanation.
- Neural Tangent Kernel (2018): a derivation that says exactly when a wide network trains like a linear model.

## Papers I disliked
- (a pattern, not a paper) A new architecture whose only argument is a leaderboard table.
- (a pattern, not a paper) A "theory" section that restates the method without predicting anything new.
