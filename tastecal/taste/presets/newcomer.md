# Newcomer

Someone new to the field who wants to understand how it got here, and what to read first.

## Problems I care about
- The few ideas the rest of the field builds on.
- Clear explanations of what each line of work is trying to fix.

## Ideas I like
- Foundational papers with one clear idea that many later works reuse.
- Surveys and analyses that explain a whole line of work.
- Benchmarks that changed what the field measures.

## Ideas I usually skip
- Small improvements to a recent method on one benchmark.
- Papers that assume a lot of background without explaining it.

## Papers I liked
- Deep Residual Learning (2015): one simple idea, a clear reason, used almost everywhere since.
- Attention Is All You Need (2017): a new building block that a whole field then adopted.

## Papers I disliked
- (a pattern, not a paper) A paper whose contribution is a 0.3-point gain on one leaderboard.
